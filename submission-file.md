## Introduction to Application

Urban FitLog is a Connected Environments fitness tracker built with Flutter for the CASA0015 module. The app addresses the question: *"How does the urban environment affect the quality and safety of outdoor exercise?"* by combining real-time onboard sensor data with live cloud API feeds.

The app integrates three onboard phone sensors — GPS (geolocator), accelerometer (sensors_plus), and pedometer — with two environmental cloud APIs (OpenWeatherMap and AQICN) and an OpenStreetMap tile service (CARTO). During a run, users see a live GPS route map, real-time air quality index, current weather conditions, step cadence derived from accelerometer peak detection, and cumulative step count. A pre-run AQI assessment tells users whether conditions are safe to exercise in.

Beyond aerobic running, the app includes a strength training logger with a 50+ exercise bilingual database, rest timer, calorie tracking, and workout templates. An activity calendar provides a monthly view of all training sessions and runs. Settings allow configuration of body data for calorie calculations, API keys, language (English/中文), and light/dark theme.
